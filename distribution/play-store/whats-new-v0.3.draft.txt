v0.3 — phone-switch day, made easy.

• Bulk backup: one file, one 8-word PAKE, every paired relationship.
  Settings → Back up all relationships. Paste on the new phone to
  bring every pairing across in one step.
• Single-relationship backup (from each contact's menu) still works
  the same way; pick whichever matches the moment.
• Payload-type dispatch on import — paste any Signet backup and the
  app routes you to the right commit flow automatically.

Upgrading from v0.2: your existing pairings migrate untouched. First
time you open Settings → Back up all relationships after updating
is a good moment to create a fresh bulk backup.

Issues: https://github.com/digital-grease/signet/issues.
