v0.3.4 — actionable crash reports, in-person pairing fix.

• In-app crash reporter: if Signet crashes, the next launch offers
  to file a pre-filled GitHub Issue with a stack trace. Crypto
  material (paired secrets, verify codes, backup payloads, contact
  labels) is redacted on-device before anything leaves your phone.
  No in-process network — "File issue" opens your OS browser.
• Fixed: in-person pairing could deadlock if one device scanned
  the other's QR before the originator scanned back. Both devices
  now wait for both scan + show before advancing.

Issues: https://github.com/digital-grease/signet/issues.
