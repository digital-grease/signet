v0.4.0: safer pairing and two-part backups.

• Both phones need Signet 0.4.0 or later to pair. Backups made with
  0.4.0 need 0.4.0 or later to restore.
• In-person pairing: one phone taps Start, the other Join.
• Long-distance pairing checks 8 words.
• Backups are two parts you keep apart: a PACKAGE file and 8 words.
• Set Signet's language on its own (Android 13+).
• Fixes to verify, restore, the clipboard and large text.

Issues: https://github.com/digital-grease/signet/issues.
